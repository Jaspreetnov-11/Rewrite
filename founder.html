My Story

Why I built this space.

in her own words

I spent years working in fitness and therapy, coaching people to strengthen their bodies and minds. But I kept seeing the same pattern: women were holding onto immense stress, keeping their struggles quiet, and feeling completely isolated even when surrounded by people.

I started Rewrite the Rules to change that. I wanted to build a circle that didn't require you to be polite or pretend you have everything together. A space that combines physical movement, art therapy, and raw, honest conversations to help you release what's holding you back.

We started locally, but the energy we've unlocked is already expanding. Whether you're here to connect, to express yourself, or simply to take a breath—you are welcome as you are.
